# Sources of the AI-family exemplars

Every figure here is the Figure 1 (or teaser) of a paper published at ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, CVPR, ACL or AAAI, 2023-2026, taken from the paper list of the Top-Conf Figure Gallery by qwdwqfwq (https://github.com/qwdwqfwq/topconf-paper-figure-gallery, MIT-licensed index). The full figures were re-extracted from the publisher's PDF or the arXiv source; 779 thumbnails, for papers whose figure we did not re-extract, were made from the gallery's own images. The figures belong to their authors and publishers (most proceedings are CC BY 4.0; AAAI keeps its own copyright); they are kept as references for study and are not to be reused as material. Each card in cards.jsonl and each manifest.json gives the paper title, the first authors and the link to the paper. Write to the maintainer of this skill to have a figure removed.
